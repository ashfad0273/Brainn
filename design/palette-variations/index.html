What the references do

visitors.now runs on two four-step ramps. Surfaces: #fff #f5f5f5 #f0f0f0 #e8e8e8 in light, and an indigo-tinted #181925 #212131 #2b2c3b #333447 on a #14141f page in dark. Text: #181925 #666 #999 #b3b3b3. Borders and hovers are black or white at 3–12% alpha (#00000014, #cccfff24), not solid greys. Most text is the darkest step, with one muted step under it. Accents (blue #00c4ff, sky #2c78fc, green, amber, red) sit in matching four-step ramps and appear only in charts and status.

godly.website now redirects to recent.design, a pure-neutral palette with zero chroma: a white page, #0b0b0b text, oklch(95.5% 0 0) and oklch(39% 0 0) as the only solid greys, and surfaces separated by black at 6–15% alpha. Saturated colour (oklch(62% .29 25) red, oklch(64% .28 350) pink, oklch(70% .27 145) green) is kept to tiny marks.

The common idea: three or four close grey steps, hairline borders that are alpha rather than solid, one strong text colour plus one muted text colour, and colour only where it means something. Brainn's light secondary text (hsl(0 0% 52%)) is 3.7:1 on white today. Every variation here lifts it above 4.5:1.
