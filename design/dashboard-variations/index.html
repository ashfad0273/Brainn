Dashboard variations

A · Bento: each widget shaped by what it holds

Deadlines

A timeline from a week ago to a month out. Each project is pinned at its due date, on the red side of Today when it's late, so you see the crunch at a glance.

To review

One big number for what's waiting, with a chip per project. Tap a chip to open that project's suggestions.

Tasks

A checklist grouped by Late, Today and This week. Tick one off right there.

Messages

The latest line of each conversation as a chat bubble, with a blue dot on unread ones. Your own replies sit on the right in blue.

B · Brief: a sentence first, then decisions

Deadlines

A one-line summary at the top, then countdown cards: a big number of days per project, coloured by how close it is.

To review

The suggestions themselves, with Accept and Ignore buttons, so you can clear them without opening the project.

Tasks

Three small lanes (Late, Today, Soon) like a mini board.

Messages

A compact inbox: who, the line, when, and unread in bold, with a Reply button on hover.

C · Calendar: plan by the next two weeks

Deadlines

A two-week strip: deadlines as coloured blocks and task due dates as small lines on their day. One week on a phone.

To review

A ring split by project with a legend, showing where the review work is piling up.

Tasks

Grouped by project, with each project's progress bar and its next two tasks underneath.

Messages

A carousel of cards, one per client, with the latest thing they said in large text. Swipe on a phone.
